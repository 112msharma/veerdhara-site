Our Story

Crafted with
Unrivalled Excellence

Veer Dhara was born from the vision of Surinder Singh — a desire to demonstrate that authenticity, diversity, tradition, and affordability can go hand in hand. From hand-carved wooden furniture to a gallery of Indian royal portraits, every detail transports you.

Our team of over six chefs, handpicked from India's finest five-star hotels, bring bold, layered flavours to every dish. Many herbs and spices are flown directly from India to guarantee absolute freshness and authenticity.

Head Chef Dharmandar Sharma · Taj Group, Jaipur · since 1988
